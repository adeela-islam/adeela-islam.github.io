Home

I am a PhD Researcher at the Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT) in Genoa, Italy, supervised by Dr. Alessio Del Bue and Prof. Dr. Vittorio Murino. Currently, I am also a Visiting Doctoral Researcher at the Max-Planck-Institut für Informatik in Germany, working with Dr. Vladislav Golyanik.

My research lies at the intersection of Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and Spatial Reasoning. I am particularly passionate about 2D and 3D reconstruction, specifically developing transformer-based models for 3D shape correspondence and curvature-guided mesh tokenization.

Previously, I worked as a Researcher and Lecturer at the University of the Punjab in Lahore, Pakistan, where I explored line extraction in handwritten documents, deep learning-based NLP, and medical image analysis.
